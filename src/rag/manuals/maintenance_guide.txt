INDUSTRIAL MAINTENANCE PROTOCOLS (AUTOMATED DIAGNOSIS GUIDE)

[OVERHEATING / TEMPERATURE RISK]
- Primary Cause: High friction, inadequate lubrication, or cooling fan degradation.
- Action: Inspect coolant flow lines, top off synthetic grease (Grade ISO VG 320), and reduce spindle load by 15%.

[BEARING / VIBRATION / TORQUE RISK]
- Primary Cause: Mechanical misalignment or gear tooth fatigue.
- Action: Perform laser alignment on drive shaft, check torque tolerances, and inspect drive belt tension.

[TOOL WEAR WARNING (> 200 units)]
- Primary Cause: Extended operation past duty cycle.
- Action: Immediately schedule tool head replacement during next scheduled downtime window.
